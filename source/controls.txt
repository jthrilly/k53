Q A light motor vehicle must have which three brakes (two may be the same brake)?
- Front brake, rear brake and engine brake
-* Service brake, parking brake and emergency brake
- Foot brake, clutch and gearbox brake
E Every light and heavy motor vehicle must have a service brake, a parking brake and an emergency brake. The parking and emergency brake may be one and the same.
R Rules of the Road 6.14.2

@ General controls
Q The "service brake" is …
-* the brake, normally a footbrake, used in the ordinary course of events to slow down or stop
- the hand brake used to keep a vehicle stationary
- any brake other than the footbrake that can stop the vehicle
E The service brake is normally the footbrake. The parking brake is normally the hand brake. An emergency brake is a brake other than the service brake that can stop the vehicle.
R Rules of the Road – definitions

E The parking brake is normally the hand brake and is used to keep a vehicle stationary.
R Rules of the Road – definitions
