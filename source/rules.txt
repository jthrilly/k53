@ Licences
Q A learner's licence is valid for …

-* 24 months
- 5 years

- To greet a friend
- To tell the car in front that the traffic light has turned green
-* Only when it is necessary for safety reasons
E No person shall use a vehicle's hooter except when necessary on grounds of safety. It must be audible from 90 m, and hooters that play a tune may not be fitted to private vehicles.

@ Towing
Q The brakes of a vehicle no longer work. If it has to be towed, it should be towed …
- with a rope, at walking pace
-* with a tow-bar (rigid connection)
- by pushing it with another vehicle
E If the towed vehicle's brakes do not comply with the law, it may only be towed if it is connected to the towing vehicle by a draw bar or tow-bar. A rope or chain would let it run into the towing vehicle.
